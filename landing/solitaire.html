How to play

Build the four foundations A → K by suit. Stack the tableau downward in alternating colors; Kings fill empty columns.

Mouse

  • Drag to move · double-click — to foundation
  • Click the stock to draw · ↻ redeals
  • ↩ Undo takes back a move

Touch

  • Drag, or tap a card then its destination
  • Double-tap — to foundation
