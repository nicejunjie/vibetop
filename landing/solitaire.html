How to play

Klondike: move all 52 cards to the four foundations, A → K by suit. On the tableau, stack downward in alternating colors; only a King may fill an empty column. Face-down cards flip when uncovered.

Mouse

  • Drag a card (or stack) to move it
  • Double-click — send a card to its foundation
  • Click the stock to draw · ↻ redeals the waste
  • ↩ Undo reverses the last move

Touch

  • Drag, or tap a card then tap its destination
  • Double-tap — send a card to its foundation
