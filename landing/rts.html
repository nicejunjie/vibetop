Iron Frontier

Build a base, mine ore, crush the enemy.

How to play

Select
Left-click a unit, or left-drag a box. Double-click selects every visible unit of that type.
Order
Right-click: move there, attack an enemy, or send a harvester to an ore patch.
Camera
WASD / arrow keys, or push the mouse to a screen edge. Click the minimap to jump.
Groups
Ctrl+1…5 stores the selection, 1…5 recalls it.
Faster
A second Barracks or War Factory speeds that queue up (20% off each, up to six — as in RA2) and gives units another exit.
Build
Pick from the right-hand panel. A finished building turns purple — click it, then click the map to place it (it must touch your base area).
Economy
Harvesters mine ore and drive it back to a refinery. More refineries, more income. Ore patches run dry — expand.
Power
Every structure draws power. Go negative and construction crawls. Bombing a power plant hurts.
Win
Destroy every enemy structure. Lose them all yourself and it's over.
Also
Space (or H) jumps back to your base · P pauses · Esc cancels placing.
