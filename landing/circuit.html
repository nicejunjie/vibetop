Circuit Runner

How to play

Run right, crush what moves, reach the uplink mast before the clock runs out. A power cell armours you up, a plasma core lets you shoot, an overclock makes you untouchable. Five sectors, one flooded with coolant, and the Mainframe waiting in sector 05.

Keyboard

  • ← → or A D — walk
  • Space / ↑ / Z — jump (hold for higher)
  • Shift / X — run, and throw fire
  • ↓ — crouch, and enter a green pipe
  • ↑ — climb a cable
  • P or Esc — pause

Touch

  • ◀ ▶ to walk, A to jump, B to run / throw fire
  • Hold A longer for a higher jump
  • ▲ climbs a cable, ▼ crouches and enters a green pipe
  • ⛶ locks landscape / fullscreen, ⏸ pauses

Worth knowing

  • Jumping while running goes further — momentum carries
  • Crush a glitch to leave its core; kick the core to bowl over a row
  • A block struck from below flips anything standing on it
  • In coolant, each press of jump is one thruster pulse — holding does nothing
  • You can't crush a coolant jelly. Shoot it, or swim around it
  • A cable unspools from one block in sector 01, and leads somewhere
  • Hidden spare units, a launch pad and a conduit are in there too
