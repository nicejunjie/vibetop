Super Vibe Bros

How to play

Run right, stomp what moves, reach the flagpole before the clock runs out. Mushrooms make you big, flowers let you throw fire, stars make you untouchable. Five worlds, including one underwater, and a boss at the end of 1-5.

Keyboard

  • ← → or A D — walk
  • Space / ↑ / Z — jump (hold for higher)
  • Shift / X — run, and throw fire
  • ↓ — crouch, and enter a green pipe
  • ↑ — climb a vine
  • P or Esc — pause

Touch

  • ◀ ▶ to walk, A to jump, B to run / throw fire
  • Hold A longer for a higher jump
  • ▲ climbs a vine, ▼ crouches and enters a green pipe

Worth knowing

  • Jumping while running goes further — momentum carries
  • Stomp a shell to stop it, kick it to bowl over a row of enemies
  • A block bumped from below flips anything standing on it
  • Underwater, each press of jump is one stroke — hold does nothing
  • You can't stomp a fish. Burn it, or swim around it
  • A beanstalk grows out of one block in 1-1, and leads somewhere
  • Hidden 1-ups, a springboard and a warp pipe are in there too
