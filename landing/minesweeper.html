How to play

Clear every safe cell — a number counts the mines around it.

Mouse & keyboard

  • Left-click — reveal · right-click — 🚩 → ? → clear
  • Both buttons or middle-click a number — open its neighbors
  • F2 or 🙂 — new game

Touch

  • Tap — reveal · long-press — flag
  • 🚩 button — taps place flags
