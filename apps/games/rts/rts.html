How to play

Touch
On a tablet: tap selects one of yours or gives the order, drag looks around, pinch zooms, a two-finger tap deselects or cancels a placement or mode, hold a cameo for its details (hold a queued one to hold or cancel it), hold Team 1/Team 2 to assign the selection. The bar's Stop and Attack-move stand in for S and Ctrl+Shift.
Select
Left-click one of your units, or left-drag a box. Double-click selects every visible unit of that type. Right-click (without dragging) deselects.
Order
With units selected, left-click: move there, attack an enemy, or send a harvester to an ore patch — as in RA2. Left-click your own Ore Refinery with a miner selected and it docks and unloads at once, however full it is — the cursor turns to the enter arrow over anything a unit can be sent into. Ctrl+click force-fires at a spot (or at your own units); Ctrl+Shift+click is an attack-move — they advance and engage whatever they meet. F then click a friendly unit to follow it.
Hold ground
S stops dead — the unit fires at what comes into range but never moves. G guards: it leaves its post by up to two cells to engage, then walks back. X scatters.
Deploy
D digs a GI in behind sandbags — longer reach and double the rate of fire, and it will not move again until you press D to pack up. The same key unfolds an MCV into a Construction Yard and empties a garrisoned building.
Camera
Right-drag (or middle-drag) grabs the map and looks around — the right button never gives an order. Arrow keys, mouse to a screen edge, or click the minimap to jump.
Teams
Ctrl+1…0 stores the selection, 1…0 recalls it, Shift+digit adds to it, Alt+digit jumps the camera to it.
Views
Ctrl+F1…F4 saves a camera bookmark, F1…F4 returns to it. N steps through everything you own. Ctrl+C saves a screenshot of the field.
Settings
Game settings on the front menu carries RA2's skirmish options — starting credits and units, game speed, Bases, Short Game, Crates, Superweapons, and your house colour out of the eight. Esc in a match pauses and opens the same card with three save slots, the speed slider and Restart.
Faster
A second Barracks or War Factory speeds that queue up (20% off each, up to six — as in RA2) and gives units another exit.
Build
Pick from the right-hand panel. A finished building turns purple — click it, then click the map to place it (it must touch your base area).
Rally
Select a Barracks or War Factory, then left-click the map: new units walk that route as they roll out. The line stays drawn while the building is selected. Click an already-selected factory again to make it primary — the one that builds.
Sidebar
Hover a cameo for its cost, power draw and prerequisites. Q W E R pick the four tabs, PageUp / PageDown scroll the list, T selects every unit of one type, K / L are repair and sell, and Power switches a structure off to free its drain.
Economy
Harvesters mine ore and drive it back to a refinery. More refineries, more income. Ore patches run dry — expand. Left-click a refinery with a miner selected to send it home early and bank what it is carrying; it goes to the one you clicked, and afterwards mines the nearest ore.
Power
Every structure draws power. Go negative and construction crawls. Bombing a power plant hurts.
Win
Destroy every enemy structure. Lose them all yourself and it's over. With Short game off you must also destroy every last enemy unit.
Also
Space jumps to the last radar event · H jumps home · Delete self-destructs the selection · P pauses · Esc cancels, and with nothing to cancel opens the options card (sound, scroll speed, restart, abort).
