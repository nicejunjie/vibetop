# Pass A (pass 3) — human-style playtest, Playwright real mouse/keyboard

Harness: chromium 1400×900 @ `deviceScaleFactor: 2` against `http://127.0.0.1:8121/rts.html`.
Every order through `page.mouse` / `page.keyboard` / real sidebar clicks. `__rtsTest` used only to read
state, move the camera, force a superweapon charge, and drop fixtures (`spawn`/`build`) so a mechanic
could be reached without a 6-minute build clock. Screenshots in this directory (~80 files).

Coverage this pass: scenario 1 (Directorate/Normal/Iron Frontier, full timed opening to defeat),
2 (every Directorate and Collective structure/defence/infantry/vehicle cameo read; full Directorate
tech base + full Soviet tech base built and used), 3 (Iron Curtain and Nuclear Missile charged and
fired; radiation layer measured), 6 (band box, single-click replace, wall drag, gate, garrison,
capture, engineer repair), 9 (Iron Frontier, River Crossing, Chokepoint Pass), 10 (menu, settings
drawer, Help, options card, save/load, Ctrl+C, sell/repair/power modes, score screen, New-mid-match).
Not reached: ore exhaustion, Hard-AI-to-the-end, Lake Divide / Frozen Front / Gem Valley,
Rocketeer/Kirov vs AA, most of the Soviet special-unit mechanics (see checklist).

## Overall feel

The world is enormously fuller than at pass 2 and most of it is *good*: a Soviet base with the statue-on-a-plinth barracks, tesla reactors and the onion-tower war factory reads as RA2 across the room; walls run out of the cursor in a proper chain; a civilian block sprouts sandbagged firing ports the moment three GIs walk into it; the nuke leaves a real crater and a green radiation field; an Engineer walks into a burning Barracks and it comes back whole; a Mirage Tank sits in your army pretending to be a tree; and the whole audio kit answers with distinct reports for every weapon. Powered and unpowered structures, rubble, LAT blends, road junctions and ore glitter all land. What has not moved is the thing that decides whether a player ever *sees* any of it: a fourth Normal match, protocol build order, both production lanes running, and I was dead at 8:48 with sixteen thousand credits I was never allowed to spend, against an AI that had built fourteen structures and fifty-nine units and out-harvested me two to one. Twenty-three credits per second on the structure lane is the whole game; until that number or the AI's wave changes, every feature above is content for a match nobody survives. Behind it sit two things a picky player hits in the first five minutes — a hover card that calls your own captured oil derrick "Enemy (Directorate)", and a deploy key that works on the button but not on the keyboard — and one thing an RA2 veteran notices immediately: on the map literally named Chokepoint Pass, the ridge across the middle is painted on, with no height on either side of it.
